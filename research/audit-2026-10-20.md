# Oct 20, 2026 audit, round 1: verification evidence

Source of the audit: `scratchpad/CLAUDE_UPDATE_INSTRUCTIONS.md` and `site_updates.json`. Both are untracked files (in `scratchpad/` and the repo root) and are not committed. Decision record: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-audit-round-1-design.md`.

## What the audit claimed and what we verified

- Every slug and every "current website" in the audit matches the repo.
- **Spam (section 1):**
  - Confirmed: Cryo Therapy, Three Peaks and Warbirds all redirect to slot-gambling sites.
  - tetonfloors.com now 301s to an unrelated site (lancastermeatmarket.com), not to "rogtotomacau". The link still goes.
- **Moved (2a):** 10 of 13 are correct. Wrong-entity targets:
  - Mountain High Mounted Shooters → usmountedshooting.com, a national association that doesn't name the club. Drop the link.
  - Teton Creative → raecreativearts.com, a different business name. Keep current link out; defer.
  - Teton Leadership Center → cwc.edu, which does mention the center. Defer to Drew.
- **Dead (2b):** confirmed via public DNS (1.1.1.1) or HTTP 404, except:
  - Farmhouse 208 is alive. Only `www.` is NXDOMAIN; `https://farmhouse208.com/` returns 200 "Farmhouse 208".
  - Teton Mountain Elk Ranch: elkadventures.com 301s to tetontrailrides.com. Possibly a rename, so defer.
  - Candidates verified 200 and naming the business: Dulce, Ollie's, Pine Needle, the library home page (Makerspace and Library), and tvhcare.org/locations (Cache Clinic).
- **https (2c):** 17 of 19 work. Keep http for Beard Outfitters (certificate fails) and Teton West Hotel (connection reset).
- **Wrong page (3):** laidbackusa.com doesn't mention a car show, Victor or Teton, so it isn't applied. The others are already "leave and flag".
- **Phone/hours/address (6):**
  - The source is the Oct 18 CSV (`research/directory-csv-review.json`), whose scraped hours `docs/EDITORIAL.md` already excluded.
  - 30 of 85 hours values are prose or blank.
  - 17 of 33 proposed addresses are for listings whose addresses `data.ts` deliberately hides (`source-conflict`).
  - Nine phone numbers are shared by 2–3 listings.
- **Descriptions (7):** 74 of 167 "replacements" are the same boilerplate, 47 are empty, and 15 are copied marketing. About 31 might be usable after rewriting.
- **Categories (8):** 50 of 64 have no concrete value ("(split: e.g. …)").
- **Additions (5):**
  - Of 160 "add" rows, about 20 duplicate existing listings and about 25 more share an existing listing's website.
  - 42 descriptions are copied marketing, and 4 mention dated events.
  - 128 rows come from welcometetonvalley.org, which has not been reviewed as a source.
- **Site-level (10):**
  - `/live/` is noindex and excluded from the sitemap by design (CLAUDE.md), so it is rejected.
  - Alt text is false: 2 of 1,207 images have empty alt in dist, and 0 of 246 on the live pages sampled.
  - Charset: `<meta charset="utf-8">` is already first. Adding `Content-Type` in `_headers` would apply to every asset under `/*`, so it is rejected.
  - Redirects go through `data/aliases.json` → `_redirects`, not `astro.config` redirects.
